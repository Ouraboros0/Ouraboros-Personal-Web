About

I am someone who loops.
Everything I touch, I return to.

My name is Gen. I build software by day and sit with my thoughts by night. I'm interested in the space between logic and feeling — the place where systems break down and something more honest begins.

This blog is not curated. It is not a personal brand. It is a place where I think out loud — about relationships, about God, about cycles I can't seem to break, about the strange beauty of watching things fall apart and choosing to rebuild anyway.

The ouroboros eats its own tail. Not out of hunger — out of necessity. The cycle is not a trap. It is the only honest map I have.
